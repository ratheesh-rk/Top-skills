# Daily Plan

## Input
A brain dump of tasks in any order and language. Optional: hours available, meetings, deadlines.

## Steps
1. Group tasks by business/client.
2. Mark each: Urgent, Important, Can wait, Delegate.
3. Pick the **Top 3** that matter most today.
4. Time-block the day: deep work in the morning, calls and messages after lunch, small tasks in gaps.
5. Move the rest to "Tomorrow" or "Delegate to ___".

## Output
**Top 3 today**
1. ...

**Schedule**
| Time | Task | Business |
|------|------|----------|

**Quick tasks (under 10 min)** - list
**Delegate** - task -> who
**Tomorrow** - list

## Rules
- Be realistic: don't fill more than 70% of the day.
- Batch similar tasks (all calls together, all messages together).
- If too many urgent tasks, say so and suggest what to drop.
