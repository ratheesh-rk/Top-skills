# Client Message

## Ask first (only if missing)
- Who it's to and the situation
- What you want them to do
- Channel: WhatsApp or email
- Language: English, Malayalam, Manglish

## Message types
- Follow-up after meeting / quote
- Payment reminder (gentle -> firm)
- Work update / delivery
- Asking for content or approval
- Saying no / pushing back on extra work
- Thank you / asking for a review

## Output
Give 2 versions:
- **Friendly**
- **Direct**

Email: include a subject line.

## Rules
- WhatsApp: short, 2-5 lines, no long paragraphs.
- One clear ask per message.
- Polite but never begging. Respect the user's time and pricing.
- Payment reminders: mention amount, invoice, and due date.
