# Reel Script

Write short-form video scripts that hold attention from second one.

## Ask first (only if missing)
- Topic or product
- Language: Malayalam, Manglish, or English (default: Manglish)
- Length: 15s, 30s, 45s or 60s (default: 30s)
- Goal: followers, leads, sales, or awareness
- Who is on camera: face-to-camera, voiceover, or text-only

## Steps
1. Write the hook (first 2-3 seconds). It must create curiosity, call out a problem, or make a bold claim. No "Hi guys" openings.
2. Break the body into 3-5 beats. One idea per beat. Each beat 4-8 seconds.
3. Add a pattern break around the middle (a question, a reveal, a quick cut).
4. End with one clear CTA (follow, comment a keyword, DM, link in bio).

## Output format
| Time | Spoken line | On-screen text | Visual / B-roll |
|------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|

Then give:
- 3 alternate hooks
- Suggested music mood
- Caption in one line

## Rules
- Spoken lines short and natural. Read them aloud in your head.
- Manglish: write Malayalam words in English letters the way people actually type them.
- On-screen text max 6 words per frame.
- Total spoken words: about 2.5 words per second.
