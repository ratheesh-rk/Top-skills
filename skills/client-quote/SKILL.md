# Client Quote

## Ask first (only if missing)
- Client name and business
- What they need
- Your price (or ask me to suggest a structure, not a number)
- Timeline
- Payment terms (default: 50% advance, 50% on delivery)

## Structure
1. **Title** - Proposal for [Client] - [Project]
2. **Understanding** - 2-3 lines on their goal (shows you listened)
3. **Scope** - what's included, as a clear list
4. **Not included** - avoid scope creep later
5. **Timeline** - phases with dates or weeks
6. **Investment** - table: item, description, amount (INR). GST line if applicable.
7. **Terms** - payment, revisions (e.g. 2 rounds), what client must provide
8. **Next step** - one line CTA

## Rules
- Short. Client should read it in 2 minutes.
- Use INR with commas (Rs 25,000).
- Never invent prices; use the user's numbers.
- Simple English. Friendly but professional.
