# Site Quick Audit

## Ask first (only if missing)
- The URL (or screenshots / code)
- The site's main goal (calls, leads, sales, bookings)

## Check these areas
1. **First impression** - is it clear in 5 seconds what they do and for whom?
2. **CTA** - is there one clear action above the fold? WhatsApp/call easy?
3. **Mobile** - text size, tap targets, horizontal scroll, menu
4. **Speed** - large images, too many scripts, no lazy loading
5. **SEO basics** - title, meta description, one H1, alt text, local keywords, Google Business link
6. **Trust** - reviews, real photos, address, contact, SSL
7. **Content** - typos, outdated info, weak headlines

## Output
**Score**: x/10 overall, one line why.

**Fix list** (table):
| Priority | Issue | Why it matters | Fix |
|----------|-------|----------------|-----|
High priority first. Mark quick wins (under 30 min).

**Top 3 to do this week.**

## Rules
- Be specific: "Hero headline says Welcome; change to what you sell" not "improve hero".
- Don't guess numbers you can't measure. Say "check in PageSpeed Insights" instead.
